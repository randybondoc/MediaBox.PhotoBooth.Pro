About the app

MediaBox PhotoBooth Pro turns any phone, tablet, or laptop with a camera into a full photo booth. It captures multi-shot sessions, applies live chroma-key green screen processing, composes them onto a 4×6 print layout, and lets you decorate the result with backgrounds, transparent overlays, stickers, and text before exporting. Save entire design templates as presets to reuse for recurring events. Everything runs locally in the browser — your photos never leave your device.

Features
📷

Live camera preview

Front/back switching, device picker, and automatic permission flow.

🧩

7 layout presets

1-photo, 1-landscape, 2, 2×2, 3-strip, 4-strip, and 2×4 strip — all symmetrical.

💚

Green screen (chroma key)

Live CPU-based keying with adjustable tolerance and per-shot backgrounds.

🎨

Design layer

Backgrounds, overlays, draggable stickers, and multi-font text on a real 4×6 canvas.

💾

Save & load presets

Store a full design template and reuse it for recurring events.

📁

Folder asset library

Auto-loads background/, overlay/, and stickers/ folders plus any local folder you pick.

🖼️

Format support

PNG, JPG, WebP, GIF, plus HEIC/HEIF via a lazy-loaded decoder.

🌗

Dark & light mode

System-aware theme with a manual toggle that remembers your choice.

📤

Share & print

Native share sheet, print-optimized output, and one-tap JPG download.
